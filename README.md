### 🌫️ US Air Pollution & Respiratory Health Web App
(Python, SQL, HTML, Google Cloud) 
Full-stack web application and interactive dashboard designed to track and display air pollution data. By integrating external air quality data with a cloud-hosted relational database, the application provides real-time insights, interactive data visualizations, and a platform for crowdsourced, user-reported county ratings.

### 🚗 NHTSA Airbag Deployment Failure Analysis
(Python, R) 
This research project analyzes factors that influence successful airbag deployment during car accidents, focusing on when and why airbags may fail to deploy. Using Random Forest and XGBoost models, we found that the initial impact point and whether a vehicle was traveling over 20 mph were among the most important factors. Our model identified airbag failures in approximately 30% of cases, compared to the 18% industry average, highlighting potential opportunities to improve vehicle safety and airbag reliability.

### 💳 Churn Prediction
(R)
This research project predicts e-commerce customer chur, comparing six classification approaches: logistic regression (best subset & LASSO), KNN, SVM (multiple kernels), and tree-based ensembles (bagging, random forest). To handle significant class imbalance (83/17 split), models were evaluated primarily on sensitivity and test error rather than accuracy. Random forest performed best, achieving a 1.46% test error and 91.4% sensitivity, while logistic regression provided interpretable insights into key churn drivers like complaints and tenure.

### 🏠 A SARIMA Model for Consumer Price Index Forecasting
(R, Excel) 
This research project models and forecasts the U.S. Consumer Price Index (CPIAUCNS, 1991–2025) using a Seasonal ARIMA approach in R. After confirming non-stationarity via the Augmented Dickey-Fuller test, log-differencing and seasonal differencing were applied, with ACF/PACF analysis and a grid search across 310 parameter combinations used to identify candidate models. The final SARIMA(0,0,2)×(0,0,1)₁₂ model was selected based on AIC and residual diagnostics, achieving a Ljung-Box p-value of 0.86 confirming white-noise residuals. The model was used to forecast 5 months of CPI values, providing a baseline tool for inflation trend analysis.
